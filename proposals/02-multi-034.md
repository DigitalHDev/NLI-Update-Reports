<!-- Title: Don't report a location move when another 034 in the same record already matches Kima -->

@GilShalit — 335 of the 1,281 "Location supposed to move X km. Manual review required."
rows are cases where the NLI record contains **more than one `034` coordinate field**, and
one of them already agrees with Kima's stored point to within 1 km. There is no move to
review: the record and Kima agree, they just disagree about which of the record's own
coordinate fields is authoritative.

I assume the runner reads one `034` — the first, or the last — and compares that. When a
record carries several (a bounding box plus a centre point, or a revised coordinate added
alongside the original), whichever one the runner picks may not be the one Kima matched.

## How I identified them

For each `location-move` case I collected every `034` in the record, computed a centre for
each, and kept the case only if at least two distinct centres exist and the *nearest* one
is more than 1 km from Kima's point. 335 cases fail that test — i.e. one of the record's
own coordinates matches Kima. The logic is in `_classify_multi_034` in `app/server.py` if
you want to check the threshold or the centre calculation.

## Proposed change

When comparing NLI coordinates against Kima's, compare against **all** `034` fields in the
record and report a move only if none of them is within tolerance.

Two details worth deciding rather than assuming:

- **Tolerance.** I used 1 km, which is arbitrary — chosen because the existing report
  threshold is 5 km and I wanted "clearly the same point", not "close". Your call.
- **Which coordinate wins when several match.** For the report this does not matter, since
  the case is dropped either way. It does matter if the runner ever writes coordinates
  back to Kima, and I do not know what it currently does there.

This is worth ~26% of the location-move rows and, together with the variant issue, about a
quarter of the whole report. It also removes rows that are actively misleading rather than
merely redundant: right now these look like 335 places where Kima's geography is wrong.
